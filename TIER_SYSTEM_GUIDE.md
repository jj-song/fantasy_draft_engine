# Fantasy Football Tier System & Draft Strategy Guide

## Overview

The fantasy football ranking system uses **Value Over Replacement (VOR)** to categorize players into strategic tiers. Each tier represents different draft approaches, risk profiles, and expected production levels.

**VOR Calculation**: `(Player Points Per Game - Replacement Level Points) × Position Scarcity Multiplier`

---

## Tier Definitions & Strategy

### 🏆 **ELITE Tier** (VOR 12+)
**Draft Rounds**: 1-4 in 12-team league  
**Player Count**: ~4 players  
**Current Examples**: 
- Saquon Barkley (RB, 16.1 VOR, 22.2 PPG)
- Jahmyr Gibbs (RB, 14.1 VOR, 20.9 PPG)  
- Bijan Robinson (RB, 12.9 VOR, 20.1 PPG)
- Derrick Henry (RB, 12.4 VOR, 19.8 PPG)

**Draft Strategy**:
- **Must-draft players** with league-winning potential
- Provide massive weekly scoring advantage (5-10+ points over replacement)
- Low bust risk, extremely high weekly ceiling
- Often position-scarce (notice all RBs in current elite tier)

**When to Target**: Never pass on these players within their draft range
**Risk Profile**: Low risk, high reward - foundational picks

---

### 💎 **PREMIUM Tier** (VOR 8-12)  
**Draft Rounds**: 5-12  
**Player Count**: ~9 players
**Current Examples**:
- Ja'Marr Chase (WR, 11.7 VOR, 23.7 PPG)
- Alvin Kamara (RB, 11.2 VOR, 18.9 PPG)
- De'Von Achane (RB, 9.2 VOR, 17.6 PPG)
- George Kittle (TE, 8.2 VOR, 15.8 PPG)

**Draft Strategy**:
- **Core roster building blocks** - reliable weekly starters
- Form the foundation of competitive fantasy teams
- Strong weekly floors with decent upside potential
- Mix of positions shows balanced scarcity

**When to Target**: Early-mid rounds, before position runs begin
**Risk Profile**: Moderate risk, consistent weekly production

---

### ⚡ **SOLID Tier** (VOR 5-8)
**Draft Rounds**: 13-30  
**Player Count**: ~17 players
**Current Examples**:
- Justin Jefferson (WR, 5.7 VOR, 18.7 PPG)
- Amon-Ra St. Brown (WR, 5.6 VOR, 18.6 PPG)
- Josh Allen (QB, 5.0 VOR, 23.3 PPG)
- Brock Bowers (TE, 7.7 VOR, 15.5 PPG)

**Draft Strategy**:
- **Quality flex plays** and weekly lineup depth
- Excellent for bye week coverage and favorable matchups  
- Good mix of floor and ceiling depending on situation
- Often includes QB1s due to position depth

**When to Target**: Mid-to-late rounds, fill roster needs
**Risk Profile**: Balanced risk/reward, matchup-dependent upside

---

### 🔧 **DEPTH Tier** (VOR 2-5)
**Draft Rounds**: 31-60+  
**Player Count**: Many players
**Current Examples**:
- CeeDee Lamb (WR, 4.3 VOR, 17.6 PPG)
- Joe Burrow (QB, 3.8 VOR, 21.9 PPG)  
- Travis Kelce (TE, 3.2 VOR, 12.2 PPG)
- J.K. Dobbins (RB, 4.9 VOR, 14.8 PPG)

**Draft Strategy**:
- **Essential bench depth** and handcuff targets
- Upside plays with proven track records
- Injury replacements and bye week fill-ins
- Often aging stars or situational players

**When to Target**: Late middle rounds, roster construction phase
**Risk Profile**: Higher variance, situation-dependent production

---

### 📋 **BENCH Tier** (VOR <2)
**Draft Rounds**: Late rounds (60+)
**Player Count**: Deep sleepers and fliers
**Examples**: Rookie WRs, backup RBs, streaming QBs

**Draft Strategy**:
- **Lottery tickets** with breakout potential
- Injury replacements and handcuffs
- Streaming options for favorable matchups
- Rookie fliers and situation-dependent plays

**When to Target**: Final rounds, after roster core is complete
**Risk Profile**: High risk, potentially high reward

## Position-Specific Tier Insights

### **Running Backs** 
- **Dominate Elite tier** due to scarcity (1.5x multiplier)
- **Injury risk** makes depth crucial
- **Workload concentration** creates clear tiers
- **Strategy**: Draft 2 RBs in first 6 rounds

### **Wide Receivers**
- **Deep talent pool** spreads across all tiers  
- **Volume-based** - target share matters most
- **Consistent scoring** with less weekly variance
- **Strategy**: Build depth, target 3-4 by round 8

### **Tight Ends**
- **Extreme scarcity** at top (1.4x multiplier)
- **Sharp drop-off** after elite players
- **Boom/bust nature** creates tier gaps
- **Strategy**: Elite TE or wait until very late

### **Quarterbacks**  
- **Deepest position** (0.9x multiplier reduces VOR)
- **High scoring** but easily replaceable
- **Streaming viable** due to depth
- **Strategy**: Wait on QB, draft in rounds 8-12

## Draft Strategy by Tier

### **Early Rounds (1-6): Elite + Premium Focus**
1. **Secure league winners** - don't overthink Elite tier
2. **Address position scarcity** - RB and TE premium  
3. **Build foundation** - target 2 Elite/Premium RBs
4. **Avoid reaches** - trust the tier system

### **Middle Rounds (7-12): Solid Tier Value**  
1. **Fill roster needs** - complete starting lineup
2. **Target upside** - boom/bust players with ceiling
3. **Position balance** - ensure adequate depth
4. **Handcuff consideration** - protect Elite RBs

### **Late Rounds (13+): Depth + Fliers**
1. **Bench construction** - injury insurance  
2. **Upside swings** - high-ceiling lottery tickets
3. **Streaming options** - QB/TE/K/DST depth
4. **Rookie investment** - future breakout candidates

---

## Advanced Tier Strategies

### **Tier-Based Drafting**
- **Stay within tiers** - avoid reaching across tier boundaries
- **Position runs** - draft ahead of runs, not during them
- **Value accumulation** - multiple Solid tier > single Premium reach

### **VOR-Based Decision Making**
- **Compare across positions** - VOR enables cross-position ranking
- **Scarcity awareness** - understand why RBs/TEs rank higher
- **Replacement level** - know when to pivot to depth

### **Risk Management by Tier**
- **Elite tier**: Maximize touches, minimize risk
- **Premium tier**: Balance floor and ceiling  
- **Solid tier**: Target specific situations and matchups
- **Depth tier**: Swing for upside, accept higher bust rate

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Reaching across tiers** - Don't draft Depth players in Premium rounds  
❌ **Ignoring positional scarcity** - RB/TE scarcity is built into tiers  
❌ **Over-drafting QBs** - Deep position doesn't require early investment  
❌ **Tier ignorance** - All Elite players are similarly valuable regardless of name recognition  
❌ **Bench construction neglect** - Depth tier players are crucial for season-long success

## Tier System Validation

The current tier cutoffs [12, 8, 5, 2] create the following distribution:

- **Elite**: 4 players (8%) - Premium draft capital
- **Premium**: 9 players (18%) - Core roster building  
- **Solid**: 17 players (34%) - Quality depth and flex
- **Depth**: ~30 players (35%) - Bench and upside
- **Bench**: Deep sleepers (5%) - Late round fliers

This distribution ensures balanced draft strategy across all phases of roster construction, from league-winners to depth pieces.

**Remember**: Tiers represent strategic value, not just projected points. A QB with 25 PPG might be Solid tier due to position depth, while an RB with 18 PPG could be Premium due to scarcity.
